---
title: Do not build a tile pipeline yet
---

Vector tiling is the most seductive piece of infrastructure in GIS. It is well
documented, intellectually satisfying, and completely unnecessary for the map
you are probably building.

## The default that is fine for longer than you think

A GeoJSON source loaded straight into MapLibre, with a demo basemap underneath.
That is it. MapLibre will happily handle a few thousand points client-side, and
clustering pushes the ceiling considerably higher:

```js
map.addSource("features", {
  type: "geojson",
  data: "/api/features",
  cluster: true,
  clusterRadius: 48,
});
```

If your dataset fits in a payload the browser can download without complaint,
you do not have a tiling problem. You have a map.

## Symptoms that mean you have earned it

Build the pipeline when you can point at one of these, measured:

- The GeoJSON payload is over ~5 MB, or first paint waits on it.
- You have polygons with enough vertices that the main thread stalls on pan.
- The data changes per-user or per-request in ways a CDN cannot cache.
- You need server-side generalisation: different geometry at different zooms.
- Someone other than your app is consuming the tiles.

Note that four of those five are about **data volume**, not features. Wanting
nicer labels is not a tiling requirement.

## What it actually costs

A tile pipeline is not one component. It is a generation step (`tippecanoe`, or
`ST_AsMVT` in Postgres), a storage decision (PMTiles on object storage, or a
live endpoint), a cache-invalidation story for when data changes, and a style
JSON that is now yours to maintain. Each of those is a thing that can break at
3am, and none of them make the first version of your map better.

## The upgrade path, when it comes

The good news is that this is a genuinely deferrable decision, because MapLibre
does not care where a source comes from. Swapping a `geojson` source for a
`vector` source is a handful of lines and a layer's `source-layer` property.
Design your layers so the filter logic reads from properties that will exist in
both, and the migration is an afternoon.

Build the afternoon's work on the afternoon you need it.
